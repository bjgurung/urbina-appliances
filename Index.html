About Us

Meet the Urbinas

Jose & Steven Urbina · Owners

Urbina Appliances Corp. is run by Jose and Steven Urbina right here in Charlotte. We specialize in used and refurbished home appliances — bringing quality machines back to life so you don't have to pay retail.

Whether you need a washer for your home, a stove for a rental, or a hard-to-find part, we're here to help. And when you buy from us, delivery & setup come at the same price — every unit is inspected and tested before it hits the floor.
